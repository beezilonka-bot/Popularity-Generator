# SKILL-X-002 — Hook Engineering

## Goal
Make the opening earn continued attention without relying on fabricated claims.

## Hook Families
- Specific result
- Surprising observation
- Contrarian interpretation
- Clear problem
- Strong question
- Curiosity gap
- Pattern interrupt
- Story opening

## Selection
Choose the hook family from the actual content and audience. Do not manufacture controversy merely to increase attention.

## Test
A hook should make clear:
1. what the post is about,
2. why it matters,
3. what tension or value justifies reading on.

## Constraint
Never create fake numbers, fake authority, fake urgency, or fake personal experience merely to strengthen a hook.
