# SKILL-X-004 — Voice & Anti-AI Writing

## Goal
Reduce generic machine-written signals while preserving clarity.

## Rules
- Prefer concrete language over abstract adjectives.
- Vary sentence length naturally.
- Use contractions when appropriate to the author's voice.
- Avoid repetitive rhetorical templates.
- Avoid inflated claims and generic motivational framing.
- Preserve distinctive vocabulary supplied by the author or source material.
- Do not impose lowercase, emojis, slang, or "edgy" tone unless supported by the requested voice.

## Editing Test
If a sentence could appear unchanged in thousands of unrelated AI posts, rewrite it around a concrete detail or remove it.
