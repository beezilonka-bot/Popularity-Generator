# SKILL-X-008 — Editing & Compression

## Goal
Increase information density without making the post unnatural.

## Passes
1. Delete redundant setup.
2. Replace weak verbs with precise verbs.
3. Remove repeated ideas.
4. Convert abstractions into concrete details when available.
5. Tighten long sentences.
6. Check opening strength.
7. Check rhythm and line breaks.
8. Recheck factual claims after edits.

## Stop Rule
Do not shorten text merely to hit an arbitrary target. Stop when every remaining sentence earns its space.
