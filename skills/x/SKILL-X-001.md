# SKILL-X-001 — X Post Fundamentals

## Goal
Write concise X content around one clear idea.

## Rules
- One post should have one primary idea.
- Lead with the highest-value or most attention-relevant statement.
- Remove setup that the reader can infer.
- Prefer concrete nouns, verbs, numbers, names, dates, and observable details when available.
- Do not invent facts, results, quotes, or personal experience.
- Match the requested voice rather than applying a generic "viral" voice.

## Output
A focused draft suitable for the requested X format.

## Avoid
Generic introductions, filler, corporate language, repetitive conclusions, and unsupported claims.
