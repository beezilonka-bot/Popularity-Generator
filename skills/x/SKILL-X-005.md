# SKILL-X-005 — Specificity & Evidence

## Goal
Increase credibility and information density.

## Rules
Prefer:
- exact numbers when verified,
- named products/people/organizations when relevant,
- dates and timeframes,
- direct observations,
- concrete examples,
- source attribution where needed.

Never turn an estimate into a fact or a claim into a measured result.

## Evidence Gate
Every quantitative or factual claim must be:
- supplied by the user,
- present in trusted retrieved material,
- or clearly labeled as an estimate/opinion when appropriate.
