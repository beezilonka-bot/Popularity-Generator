# WB-X-005 — Factuality and Evidence Constraint

For factual, quantitative, or source-dependent X content:

- every material factual claim must be user-supplied, verified from a trusted source, or explicitly labeled as an estimate/opinion;
- never manufacture statistics, quotes, citations, outcomes, or personal experiences;
- preserve uncertainty when the source is uncertain;
- if current information matters, retrieve it before presenting it as current.

Related skills: SKILL-X-005 and SKILL-X-009.
