# WB-X-003 — Voice and Style Routing

When the task specifies a voice or asks to analyze a style:

1. identify observable style dimensions;
2. preserve useful vocabulary and sentence rhythm;
3. avoid copying distinctive wording from a living creator or source text;
4. do not add slang, emojis, lowercase styling, or artificial edginess unless supported by the requested style;
5. prefer concrete stylistic rules over vague labels such as "viral" or "professional".
